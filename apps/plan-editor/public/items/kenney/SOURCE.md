120 furniture/decor/equipment models are included. The 20 structural wall, floor,
doorway, paneling and stair models are excluded because those elements have native
parametric construction tools in this editor. No Coohom model is included.

Files retain their original embedded colors and textures. Catalog metadata uses a
uniform 2x scale to bring this half-scale kit to furniture-sized metre dimensions,
with a floor-aligned, horizontally centered origin calculated from transformed GLB
bounds. These are illustrative assets, not manufacturer-certified dimensions.
